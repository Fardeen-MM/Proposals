  • Trust scales through teaching, not slogans. Patent law isn't a side-of-the-bus business. People hire the attorney whose head they trust to be inside the invention with them. The webinar is the cheapest possible way to demonstrate that, at scale.

✓ Free consultations that arrive pre-educated — not tire-kickers with a trademark +

You said qualified-referral services were a "net loss" because they sent you people who'd take a half hour of your time and then turn out to want a trademark. The webinar fixes that by making the prospect spend the half hour before they ever see your calendar.

The qualification flow:
  • 30–45 minutes of education first: Anyone booking a free consult has already watched you teach. They know what a patent is, what it costs, how long it takes, and whether their idea is even patent-eligible. Trademark-seekers self-select out before they reach you.
  • Pre-consult intake form: Before the call, we capture their technology area, stage of invention, and a 2–3 sentence summary — non-confidential level, no NDA needed, just enough for you to know whether to take the call yourself or hand it to the right specialist.

The Honest Take

Where this funnel underperforms, and what we won't pretend.

This isn't a magic wand. Patent law is a longer sales cycle than the immigration vertical in the benchmark above — an inventor who watches your webinar in May might not file with you until August, September, or later. If you're expecting same-month payback like the immigration client, that's not realistic for your vertical.
