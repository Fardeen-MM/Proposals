What You Get

✓ A webinar that runs itself, every day, in 3 languages +

You do the live webinar one time per language. After that, the system runs it daily, automatically, for every new lead — while you're sleeping, with clients, or on vacation.

How it works:
  • You record once, live: One Hindi webinar, one Tamil webinar, one English webinar. 45 minutes each. Done in your first two weeks with us.
  • System runs them on loop: Every new lead picks a slot ("starts in 7 minutes" / "starts in 1 hour"). They watch what feels like a live event — with a chat, a host, a Q&A. It converts like live because to them, it is.
  • Language-matched targeting: Hindi speakers see Hindi ads → Hindi webinar. Tamil speakers see Tamil ads → Tamil webinar. English speakers see English ads → English webinar. No mixing, no friction.
  • Auto-reminders: SMS, email, and WhatsApp before the webinar. Show rates around 50% (industry average for free webinars is 30-35%).
Why this beats running ads straight to a consultation booking:
  • Cold traffic doesn't book paid consultations. They need to know who you are, that you're real, that you're qualified.
  • The webinar does that work for you — positions you as the expert, educates them on programs they're eligible for, builds trust.
  • By the time they hit the "book consultation" button, they've already spent 45 minutes with you.

The Caribbean RCIC agency: 7,000 webinar registrations in 30 days, 180 paid consultations booked, $47,700 in consultation revenue alone.

✓ Paid consultations only — no more free advice +

Right now you're not charging much for consultations. That's leaving money on the table and attracting the wrong people. This system flips it.

The payment flow:
  • $150 CAD per consultation communicated clearly at the end of the webinar (we'll test $150 to start — can move to $200 once volume is proven).
  • Payment link sent immediately: Stripe checkout, secure, mobile-friendly. They pay before they pick a calendar slot.
  • No payment, no booking: The calendar literally cannot lock until payment clears. No-shows drop dramatically because they've already paid.
  • Auto-confirm: Payment clears → calendar invite, confirmation email, WhatsApp message all fire. Hands-off.
Why this works for your practice:
  • Tire-kickers screen themselves out. The people on your calendar paid $150 to talk to you — they're serious.
  • You make money on Day 1. Even before a single retainer signs, the consultation revenue alone covers the system cost. Most months it's pure profit on consultations.
  • Your time is protected. No more 30-minute calls with people who just wanted free advice and disappeared.

At $150 × 50 paid consultations a month = $7,500/month in consultation revenue alone, before a single retainer signs. The Caribbean agency hit 180 in their first month.

What we track:
  • Cost per webinar registrant — per language, per country

  • Cost per paid consultation — the real conversion number

  • Full CRM stack: We set you up on a CRM that tracks every lead from registration through retainer signing, with WhatsApp, SMS, and email automations built in.
  • Closed-loop reporting: When you mark someone as retained, we trace it back to the exact ad in the exact language that brought them.
Why this matters for scaling:

Why a webinar — can't we just run ads to a booking page? +
Cold traffic doesn't pay $150 to a stranger. They need to know who you are, see your face, hear you explain a program they're eligible for, and trust you. The webinar does that work in 45 minutes. Skip the webinar and you'd need 5-10x the ad spend to get the same paid bookings — or you get bookings from people who never paid.

$150 is the recommendation based on what's working in similar markets, but it's flexible. Some clients run $99 to start, scale up to $200 once volume is proven. The system supports A/B testing on the price — we can run two prices in parallel and let the data decide.
