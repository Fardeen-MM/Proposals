  • Cost per signed retainer: what it costs to convert into a paying retainer client

Questions

I have no website, no Instagram, no online presence. Will this still work? +
Yes. You'll need to buy a domain (GoDaddy or Namecheap, ~$15/year), set up a basic Facebook page, Instagram page, Meta business manager, and Google business profile — we'll guide you through each. Then we build a basic homepage and the Korean landing pages on your domain. The ads themselves create the audience — no following needed. Get a handful of intro posts up on Instagram so the profile doesn't look empty, and you're ready.
What's a realistic number for someone starting from zero presence? +
40–50 paid consultations per month is a conservative starting point for month one. That's the floor we've seen across clients launching cold. Given that you only want five to ten retained cases a month, having 40–50 prepaid consults at the top of the funnel means you'd be selecting from a strong pool — not chasing volume.
Can the Korean funnel target Koreans outside Korea? +
Yes — and we should. There are large Korean communities in Hong Kong, Taiwan, Vietnam, Australia, the UK, and across continental Europe. Those audiences often convert better because they've already left home once and are more open to a second move. We typically run one campaign targeting Korea directly and a second targeting Korean speakers internationally, then optimize toward whichever audience converts more efficiently.
I want to commit at least six months. Is that realistic? +
That's exactly the horizon this is built for. Month one is launch and learn. Months two and three are optimization — finding which Korean ad creative, which audience segments, and which webinar angles convert best. Months four through six are scaling what works. If we're at 30–50 paid consults a month by month three and you're closing five quality retainers, the math takes care of itself. If we're not, we keep adjusting until we are.
How will I manage 50+ consultations as a solo practitioner? +
Two levers, both yours. First, the consult price is the throttle — at $200 CAD you get one volume; at $250 you get a lower volume of more committed people; at $300 it slows further. Second, we can pause ads any day, any time. The funnel runs on your schedule. You said you wanted to slow down, not be pulled back into corporate-style volume. This system respects that — you control the tap.
What if I want to cancel? +
30 days written notice. No penalties. You keep the Meta business manager and ad account (in your name, your billing), the Meta pixel and all its conversion data, the audience the algorithm has learned, your domain, and every Korean-speaking lead in the pipeline — fully exportable. If you want to keep using the CRM, you can retain it on a monthly subscription — priced at the time of cancellation based on your usage and number of users.
How will Meta charge my card? +
If your Meta ad account is brand new, expect frequent small charges (every $5–$60) in the first few weeks — Meta does this to verify the card. After ~30 days of clean billing, charges consolidate to once-weekly or once-monthly. Give your credit card company a heads-up, and consider using a points card (Aeroplan/Amex) so you earn travel rewards on every dollar of ad spend. Meta issues a clean monthly invoice for your bookkeeper.
