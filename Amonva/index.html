What You Get

✓ A webinar funnel that runs every day, 24/7 +

You record the webinar once. We run it for you every day, in every time zone, until people book a paid consultation.

How it works:
  • Live launch: You host one 45-minute webinar live. We help you build the slides, the script, and the offer. You present, we handle the tech, registration, and reminders.
  • Evergreen replay: We record that live session and run it as an "on-demand" or "starting in 8 minutes" webinar every single day after that. Leads can register and watch within minutes — no waiting a week for the next live.
  • Daily 7:30pm replay: For people who want to watch later, we run the same recording every evening at 7:30pm local — after work, after dinner, when they're ready to focus.
  • You only do this once: The live webinar is the one heavy lift. After that, the system runs without you presenting again.
What we build for you:
  • Landing pages: Two pages — one to register for the webinar, one for the post-webinar paid consultation booking.
  • Webinar tech stack: Registration system, automated reminder sequences, live and evergreen player.
  • Email + SMS + WhatsApp automations: Confirmations, reminders 24 hours before, 1 hour before, "you're missing it" pings. Multi-channel because immigration leads check WhatsApp more than email.
  • The paid consultation booking flow: After watching, viewers see a booking page that requires payment to lock in a slot — no payment, no booking.
Practice areas we'd lead with:
  • Outland advertising — lower cost per lead, higher conversion. Express Entry, PNP, study permits aimed at skilled professionals abroad.
  • Inland follow-up — spousal sponsorship, humanitarian, work permit extensions advertised to people already in Canada.
  • Refugee work — we typically do not advertise this on paid funnels (different client journey, different ethics around marketing). We'd discuss this in discovery.

A Canadian RCIC consultancy running this exact funnel since March 23 has — in 5 weeks — generated 8,500 leads, booked 174 paid consultations at $265 each, signed 7 retainers, and tracked $70,000 CAD in revenue on roughly $3,000 in ad spend.

✓ Only paid consultations get on your calendar +

Your time is the most expensive thing in this business. Free consultations waste it. Every slot on your calendar should be paid before it's confirmed.

The booking flow:
  • Price stated upfront: The post-webinar booking page clearly displays the consultation fee — no surprises, no haggling.
  • Payment required to book: Stripe (or your processor of choice) holds the slot only after payment clears. The calendar invite doesn't fire, the confirmation doesn't send, the slot isn't held until the money lands.
  • Automated confirmations: Once paid — calendar invite, email confirmation, SMS reminder, WhatsApp follow-up. All automated.
  • Reminders that prevent no-shows: 24-hour, 1-hour, and 15-minute reminders across SMS, email, and WhatsApp. Show rates jump from 50–60% to 85–95%.
Pricing strategy — you have options:
  • Stay at $150 CAD: You keep $105 per consultation after our 30% commission. Volume goes from ~20/month to 60—100/month.
  • Raise to $200 CAD: You keep $140 per consultation (close to your current $150 net) but commission covers our cost. This is what we typically recommend.
  • Tiered pricing: $200 for in-Canada consults, $300+ for international. Reflects the actual complexity of the work and lets you capture more value from outland clients.
Why this works for a sole practitioner:

You said you can do up to 20 consultations a month at full capacity. The system is designed to fill that capacity first, not flood you with volume you can't handle. Once you're at 20/month and signing 8–10 retainers, that's your signal to hire support — and the revenue from the system funds it.

At $200 CAD × 20 paid consultations/month = $4,000 in consultation revenue alone, before a single retainer is signed.

  • Every lead has a full timeline: which ad they clicked, when they watched the webinar, what they paid, who they are, when they last engaged.
  • You log in, see exactly where each lead is in the journey, and never lose track of a follow-up.
Why this matters when you scale:

Once we know your true cost per retainer is, say, $300 and your average retainer is $3,000, the math gets simple:

  • Want 5 more clients next month? Invest $1,500.
  • Want 10 more? Invest $3,000.
  • Want to fund hiring a junior consultant or assistant? You know exactly what ad spend justifies it.

No more guessing where your clients come from. No more wondering if Facebook is "working." You'll have one dashboard with one number: cost per retained client.

Honest answer: no — not at the start. NC Canada has the capacity to handle 100+ consultations because they have multiple consultants. We'd target filling your 20-slot capacity first, then plan together for hiring help. We'll commit to a minimum number of paid consultations during discovery once we understand your target markets.
What's the total cash I'm committing in month one? +
$1,000 to us (one-time, on signing) + $1,800 to Meta over the first month (daily ad spend) + ~$400 in CRM execution costs = ~$3,200 CAD total in month one. After month one, your monthly cash exposure drops to ~$2,200 ($1,800 ad + $400 CRM). The commission to us only kicks in after revenue is generated.
What if I need to delay because of the paralegal course? +
Completely fine. We can hold the start date until you're ready financially. The proposal stays valid — we'll lock in the same terms whenever you're ready to sign. Just give us roughly 2 weeks of build time before you want to be live.
What about my refugee work? +
Refugee claims have a different ethical posture — we generally don't run paid acquisition funnels for refugee work because it's a vulnerable client base and the legal framework around solicitation is sensitive. Your refugee practice would continue running as it does today (referrals, organic, professional networks). The paid funnel focuses on Express Entry, PNP, study/work permits, spousal, and humanitarian.
What if I want to cancel? +
30 days written notice. No penalties, no early-termination fees. We pause your campaigns, you stop being charged for ad spend, and the partnership ends cleanly. Whatever revenue has already landed in your account is yours.
Can we negotiate the retainer commission? +
We're at 20% on retainers in this proposal — already negotiated down from our standard 30%. We're firm on the 30% paid consultation commission since that's the volume play, but we have flexibility on retainer % depending on case complexity (refugee & humanitarian retainers, for example, take longer and we'd discuss those separately).
