  • Runs on your pages: Every ad publishes from the Jacks Education Facebook and Instagram accounts, so the following you build is yours and compounds after the campaign.
  • No landing page friction: The enquiry form opens inside Facebook or Instagram. Name, contact, prior study, course interest, funding status — filled in without ever leaving the app.

The evergreen webinar model is what put 8,500 registrations in 30 days through Northern Connections Canada Immigration. Different sector, same machine — and the reason we're not starting there is timing, not doubt.

✓ Automated messaging that reaches the student before you can dial +

Someone scrolling Instagram fills in your form because at that exact moment the idea of studying in the UK feels possible. Ten minutes later they've seen four other things and forgotten you entirely. The messaging exists to hold that moment open until you get to the phone.

What fires automatically:
  • Within two minutes: WhatsApp, email, Facebook Messenger and Instagram DM all go out, thanking them and opening with a question — have you studied at university before, when are you looking to start.
  • The question is doing work: A reply tells you their intent before you dial, so you already know whether you're talking about funding, course fit, or eligibility when they pick up.
  • AI follow-up: If you haven't replied yet, the bot keeps the conversation moving rather than letting it die in an unanswered thread. It hands over the moment you step in.
  • Multi-day sequence: Non-responders keep receiving structured follow-ups across channels rather than being written off after one attempt.
What is still on you:

The messaging buys you minutes, not hours. Enquiries land in your email and your phone as they arrive, and the call still has to happen inside five minutes, roughly 9am to 8pm. You've told me you'll do it in three. That single habit is the difference between this campaign working and this campaign being an expensive lesson — and it's the exact place you watched GCC lose students while you were subletting their office.

Multi-channel nurture is why Northern Connections converted 160+ registrations into paid consultations in a single month. Registrations don't convert. Follow-up converts.

Questions

Whose Facebook and Instagram pages do the ads run on?+
Yours. Every ad publishes from the Jacks Education accounts, so the followers and engagement accumulate to your brand rather than ours. The Meta business manager, ad account and pixel are all set up in your name with your billing from day one.
Do I own the CRM?+
Your data does — every enquiry, contact record, note and document is yours and exportable at any time. The pipeline structure, automation logic and nurture sequences we build are Mortar Metrics intellectual property and stay with us. If you cancel, you keep all your data, and continued access to the CRM itself is available on a monthly subscription priced at that point based on your usage and user count.
What happens if I cancel?+
Thirty days' written notice, no penalty. You keep the Meta business manager and ad account, the pixel and everything it has learned, your domain, and every enquiry the campaign generated. CRM access continues by subscription if you want it; your data is exported to you either way.
How will Meta charge my card?+
A new ad account gets charged frequently and in small amounts at first — every £5 to £50 — while Meta verifies the card. After about 30 days of clean billing it consolidates to weekly or monthly. Warn your bank so nothing gets blocked, and use a points card if you have one. Meta issues a clean monthly invoice for your accountant.
When do we build the webinar?+
After September. The webinar — recorded once, running daily, ending in a free consultation booking — is a strong asset and it's where a lot of the money in this model sits. But it takes time to build and refine, and you don't have that time before the October intake. We secure your contracts first, then build it for January.
What if the enquiries are poor quality?+
Some will be, and that's inherent to instant forms — the form is easy to fill in, so people fill it in casually. It's the trade for volume and cheap data in a two-month window. Speed is the filter: a casual enquirer called within three minutes often turns out to be serious, and the same person called the next day never answers. We refine the targeting weekly from what the data shows.
What do I need to provide?+
Access to your Meta business manager (or we set one up in your name), your page access, your website domain for pixel installation, details of your university contracts and entry requirements, and a few hours in week one. After that, your availability on the phone.
