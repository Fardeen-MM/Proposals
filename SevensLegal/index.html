✓ Case-specific qualification for every practice area +

A DUI inquiry and an assault charge couldn't be more different. The AI knows that — and asks the right questions for each.

For DUI / DWI inquiries:
  • Timeline: When did the stop happen?
  • Evidence: Breath or blood test? Know the result?
  • History: First offense or prior DUI?
  • Urgency: Court date or DMV hearing scheduled?
For domestic violence:
  • Status: Have they been arrested or charged?
  • Protective order: Is there one in place?
  • Urgency: Report date or arraignment coming up?
  • Representation: Do they have a public defender already?
For drug offenses, theft, fraud, assault:
  • Each case type gets its own question flow
  • Felony vs. misdemeanor flagged automatically
  • Jurisdiction check (San Diego County confirmed)
  • Urgency and timeline captured
Lead scoring built in:
  • Court date within 7 days: +25 points — highest urgency
  • Currently in custody: +20 points — family calling, willing to pay
  • Felony charge: +15 points — higher case value
  • No existing attorney: +10 points — ready to hire
  • Already has private attorney: -10 points — likely price shopping
  • Out of jurisdiction: -25 points — can't serve

Hot leads (70+ score) get instant alerts to Stan. Warm leads (40-69) get booked within 24 hours. Cold leads get nurtured automatically.

Every practice area gets its own qualification flow. You tell us the criteria, we build the conversation.

✓ Full-Funnel Tracking +

You know your cost per call is $142. But that's like a restaurant counting how many people looked at the menu. How many actually sat down and ordered?

  • Impressions (how many people saw the ad)
  • Clicks (how many clicked)

Then they hand you a PDF and say "we got you 200 leads this month!" But how many became retainers? They don't know. They don't track that far.

What we track:
  • Lead → Contact rate: Did we actually reach them?
  • Contact → Qualified rate: Were they worth talking to?

Your weekly report includes:
  • Leads generated (by case type, by campaign, by ad)

  • Qualification rate and common disqualifiers
  • Booking rate and drop-off reasons
  • Show rate and no-show patterns
  • Close rate and revenue generated
  • True CPA and ROI by case type

This is how we know the difference between a $37 cost per lead and an $800 cost per retainer. Most agencies would've only reported the $37.
