How it works:
  • Instant trigger: The moment a lead submits a form, our system fires. Within 30 seconds, they receive a call from your Voice AI assistant calling on behalf of Tarek Associates.
  • Staggered retry logic: No answer at 5pm? The system calls again at 6pm the next day. Then 7pm the day after. Different times because people have schedules — night shifts, commutes, putting the kids to bed.
  • Parallel SMS and WhatsApp: Every call attempt fires a simultaneous message: "Hi, this is Tarek Associates. We just tried calling about your enquiry. Reply YES if now is a good time." Covers the people who don't answer unknown numbers.
  • Until 9pm, 7 days a week: Your students are often working during the day. They submit forms on their lunch break or in the evening. We call them when they're actually free.
What changes for your consultants:

Right now your team starts every day with a cold list. They spend the first four hours of their day chasing people at the top of the funnel. With Voice AI handling all of that, your consultants arrive to a calendar with qualified, confirmed appointments already booked. They focus on closing — not chasing.

What happens on the call:
  • AI introduces itself as calling from Tarek Associates
  • Confirms they submitted an enquiry and asks when they're free to talk briefly
  • Walks through 4–5 qualification questions (customised to your criteria)
  • If qualified: books directly into the appropriate consultant's calendar
  • Sends confirmation via SMS, email, and WhatsApp
  • If not qualified: routes into a separate nurture pipeline for manual follow-up

Speed-to-lead studies show calling within 5 minutes makes you 100x more likely to connect vs. calling after 30 minutes. Right now you're calling after 1,440 minutes. We call in under 1.

✓ Only SFE-eligible, course-matched students reach your consultants +

Of your 1,200 monthly leads, roughly 1,000 won't enrol. Your consultants are currently calling all 1,200 to find the 200 who will. That's expensive, demoralising, and completely fixable.

The qualification checks:
  • SFE claimed before: The most important disqualifier. AI confirms this directly and immediately. Those who have already claimed go into a separate pipeline — they don't reach your consultants.
  • Course match: AI confirms the course they expressed interest in is one you offer, and that they meet basic eligibility criteria.
  • Location verification: Confirms they're in London, Birmingham, Ilford, or another area you serve — not just someone who accidentally landed on your ad from Manchester.
  • Enquiry intent: AI distinguishes between "just looking" and "ready to enrol" — flags urgency so consultants can prioritise.
What happens after qualification:
  • Passes all checks: Booked directly into a consultant's calendar with full case notes pre-populated. Consultant sees name, course interest, location, SFE status, and any other answers before the call.
  • Fails one check: Moves into a nurture sequence. Gets added to the manual follow-up pipeline for your consultants to revisit. Not lost — just not prioritised.
  • Uncontactable after 4 attempts: Moved to a "cold" pipeline. Your consultants can still work it, but it's clearly labelled and doesn't clog the main funnel.
The net effect:

Your consultants stop spending 80% of their time on the wrong leads. They see a calendar of pre-qualified students who confirmed eligibility, confirmed course interest, and actually picked up the phone. Their close rate improves because they're only talking to the right people.

You told me your qualification question on the form is "Have you claimed SFE before — Yes/No." About half of people who answer "No" on a form will say something different when a human (or Voice AI) asks them directly. Real-time qualification catches this before your consultants waste their time.
