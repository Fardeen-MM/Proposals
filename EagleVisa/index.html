✓ Paid consultations that book themselves — price as your throttle +

You don’t want 400 leads a month again. You want the right number of serious bookings at a price that respects your time.

The booking flow:

  • $350 CAD — quarter of the volume, more revenue per booking, only the most committed get through.

You pick the price. We adjust the webinar and landing page language accordingly. If you’re overloaded, we raise it the same week.

Why a webinar instead of just ads + a form? +
