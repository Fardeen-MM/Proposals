Why Mortar Metrics

✓ Full-funnel tracking, not cost-per-lead theatre +

Most agencies report "cost per lead" and call the month done. That's like a restaurant counting how many people walked past the door instead of how many sat down and ordered.

What we track (that most don't):
  • Lead → Contact rate: Did we actually reach them?
  • Contact → Qualified rate: Were they worth talking to?
  • Qualified → Booked rate: Did they pay and schedule?
  • Booked → Show rate: Did they show up?
  • Show → Close rate: Did they retain?
Why this changes everything:

When the data shows booking rate is strong but show rate is weak, we know exactly where to fix. Maybe reminder sequences need work. Maybe we're scheduling too far out. Maybe one geography has cultural friction with the confirmation flow.

When one ad variant converts leads to retainers at 10% and another at 3%, we kill the loser and scale the winner — based on signed retainers, not cost per lead.

This is how we know Kay's true CPA is roughly $160 per retained client — not the $0.60 cost per lead that most agencies would have reported and left you to guess about.
