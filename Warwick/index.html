Questions

What if I want to cancel? +
30 days written notice. No penalties. We'll work with you to ensure a smooth wind-down of active campaigns and any paid consultations already in your pipeline.
Why 25% on retainers and not just consults? +
Because the funnel is what generates the retainer, not just the consult. Without the webinar, that nurse in Manchester never books with you. Without the paid consult, she never signs a retainer. Our job is the whole journey, so our fee reflects the whole journey. First retainer only — after that, they're 100% yours for life.
How will Meta charge my card? +
If your Meta ad account is brand new, expect frequent small charges (every $5-$60) in the first few weeks — Meta does this to verify the card. After ~30 days of clean billing, charges consolidate to once-weekly or once-monthly. We'd recommend giving your credit card company a heads-up and using a points card (Aeroplan/Amex) so you earn travel points on every dollar of ad spend. Meta also issues a clean monthly invoice for your bookkeeper.
What do I need to provide? +
Access to your Meta business manager (or we help you set it up), access to Clio, your payment provider of choice (LawPay, Clio Payments, or Stripe all work), subject-matter input for the webinar, and 4-6 hours of your time in week 1 for discovery and webinar prep. That's it.
Will I know if it's working before the money adds up? +
Yes. Within three weeks of launch, we'll have enough data to tell you honestly whether the funnel is worth continuing. Registration numbers, show rates, and early paid-consult conversions are leading indicators we can read at week 2-3. If the signs are wrong, we'll say so ourselves — you shouldn't have to guess.
