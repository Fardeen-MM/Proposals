What You Get

✓ A webinar in Farsi, Arabic, and English — built for Gulf-based skilled workers and students +

Most people in the Gulf don’t know exactly what they qualify for. They’ve heard of Express Entry. They haven’t heard of PNP, SINP, OINP, or the student-to-PR pathway that fits their exact profession. A webinar shows them — and self-deselects the people who shouldn’t be on a paid call with you anyway.

Why a webinar, not just ads to a form

An ad with a form only catches people already searching. A webinar creates new demand and educates in real time. By the time a viewer finishes 45 minutes, they’ve self-assessed against the criteria you’ve laid out — the ones who shouldn’t pay, don’t pay. The ones who should are already convinced you’re the right person.

What we build for you
  • Meta ad campaigns in Farsi, Arabic, and English targeting nurses, engineers, teachers, tradespeople, IT professionals, healthcare workers, and recent graduates across the Gulf. You tell us which countries to include and which to exclude.
  • Pre-qualified targeting at the ad level — profession, age range, education stage. The webinar audience is already inside the eligibility zone before they sign up.
  • A landing page that lets them register and start watching within minutes, or pick a fixed daily slot (e.g., 7:30pm Gulf time). When the lead clicks, they can be inside the webinar almost immediately.
  • A 32-slide webinar deck and script, co-built with you. You record it live once with a real audience. After that, it runs pre-recorded every day, looking live to every attendee.
The webinar narrative (modeled on what works)
  • Open with audience interaction — where they’re from, what they do
  • Establish what they’ll learn in 45 minutes: which pathway fits, whether they qualify, what to do in the next 30 days
  • Brief intro to who you are — 30 years of practice, RCIC, the work you’ve done
  • Canada’s targets and prioritized sectors over the next 3 years
  • Example pathways by profession (nurse, engineer, tradesperson, recent grad)

  • What to do in the next 30 days to start an application

  • Pricing of the consultation, how to book, fee credits toward retainer
  • Live Q&A and recap

Questions

What if a paid consultation reveals someone has no real pathway? +
They still get value. Every consultation produces a real CRS reading, an honest pathway assessment, and a 30/60/90-day plan to improve their score (IELTS, second degree, additional work experience). The fee credits toward a future retainer if they qualify later. The webinar and ad targeting also pre-filter heavily, so most viewers who reach the booking stage are already inside the eligibility zone. In Kay’s reference funnel, ~5% of paid consultations convert to retainers immediately. The rest still walk away with something they paid for, not nothing.
Why focus on the Gulf specifically? +
Three reasons. (1) The Gulf has large concentrations of both Iranians and Arabic speakers, so one ad budget reaches two communities. (2) Many Gulf residents are skilled workers (nurses, engineers, IT, healthcare) on temporary visas already looking for permanent options — high-intent audience. (3) Recent grads from Gulf universities are strong fits for student-to-PR pathways. We can include or exclude specific countries (UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman) based on your preference.
Can the webinar be just student visas and visitor visas, not PR? +
Yes. The webinar narrative is built with you, around the pathways you actually want to deliver on. We can lead with student visas and visitor visas, with PR mentioned as a longer-term option for genuinely eligible profiles. The ad targeting will be tuned to match — recent grads, prospective students, family-visit applicants. As Canada’s policies and your team’s preferences shift, we can update the deck and re-target without rebuilding the system.
What does the AI chatbot actually do, and what doesn’t it do? +
It nurtures leads and books paid consultations — that’s the entire job. It does not give immigration advice, calculate CRS scores, or assess eligibility. If a lead asks something the chatbot isn’t scoped to answer, it routes the conversation to your team with full context. This was Paria’s explicit concern, and the system is designed around it.
What does our team need to provide? +
Willingness to record the webinar live once per language (~45 minutes each in Farsi, Arabic, English — whoever on your team is most comfortable presenting in each language), input on which Gulf countries to include or exclude, qualification criteria for paid consultations, and weekly feedback on lead quality. We handle campaign setup, ads, copy in all three languages, creative, tracking system build, chatbot, Stripe integration, and reporting.
What if we want to cancel? +
30 days written notice. No penalties, no exit fees. The $1,000 build fee is non-refundable. The leads we generated stay yours — you keep the ad account, landing page, webinar recording, CRM data, and lead list. You can keep following up on them on your own time after we part.
Why $1,500 minimum ad spend? +
Below $50/day, Meta’s algorithm doesn’t get enough signal to learn who to show your ads to — which actually makes ads more expensive per booking, not cheaper. $1,500/mo is the floor where the system can learn properly. As results come in, you can choose to increase spend to scale, or keep it at the floor and use consultation price to control volume.
