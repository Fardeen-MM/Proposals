I've never done a webinar before. What do I actually need to do? +
One live recording session, 45 minutes on camera. We script the content with you, build the slides, run the technology, manage the audience and the chat, and coach you through delivery. What's my total upfront cost in the first month? +
$1,000 build fee paid to us on signing, $1,800 paid to Meta over the first month for ad spend, and ~$300–500 in CRM execution costs (SMS, WhatsApp, email firing) that go directly to the platform. Total cash exposure in month one is roughly $3,300 USD. After month one, your monthly recurring cost drops to ~$2,200. Performance fees only kick in after revenue is generated — if the system books no paid consultations, you owe us nothing beyond the build fee.
Why didn't my Instagram and Google Ads work before? +
You said it yourself — you didn't put real money behind either, so the data wasn't enough to draw conclusions. But there's a deeper issue. Direct ads send people to a contact form, where they're asked to commit to a phone call with a lawyer they've never heard of. The conversion rate is low and the leads are cold. The webinar is the bridge: the prospect gives you 45 minutes of attention before they're asked for anything, and by the time they see the consultation offer they already trust you.
What if Maritza and I can't handle the volume? +
Volume is controlled by consultation price, not ad spend. If your calendar fills up, we raise the consultation price within the same week — fewer bookings, similar gross revenue, higher-intent prospects only. Lowering ad spend is the wrong lever (it makes Meta's algorithm stall). Pricing is the right lever, and we can move it any time. You're never in a situation where the system outpaces what you can comfortably handle.
Can we focus on US-based clients instead, since most of mine are inland? +
Eventually yes — but starting outland is faster, cheaper, and more profitable. Inland CPA in the US averages $300–600+ per lead because the audience is competitive and the keywords are expensive. Outland (advertising in Latin America to bring people into the US) typically runs $5–30 per booked paid consultation. Once outland is generating consistent revenue, we use that cash flow to fund inland campaigns — spousal, AOS, citizenship — which cost more per lead but bring you the in-US clients you're already used to. The end state is both engines running.
How does the 25% performance fee actually work? +
You set the consultation price (recommended $200 USD). Stripe collects payment into your account. We invoice you 25% of the consultation revenue and 25% of the first retainer per lead, after the revenue has landed. If a client comes back six months later for a second matter, we take nothing on that. If a referral comes through Maritza's network independent of the funnel, we take nothing on that either. We only earn on revenue the funnel directly generates, the first time it generates it.
What if I want to cancel? +
30 days written notice. No penalties, no exit fees. The $1,000 build fee is non-refundable (it's paid out in work delivered). We wind down active campaigns cleanly and ensure any paid consultations already on your calendar are honored. That's it.
