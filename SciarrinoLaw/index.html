What You Get

✓ An educational IP webinar funnel that runs 24/7 after one live session +

You teach once. We run it forever. Every day a new audience of founders, creators, and brand owners learns they have IP worth protecting — from you.

The structure:
  • Live webinar (once): You present a 30–45 minute session — when to trademark, how clearance searches work, copyright and DMCA basics, how to choose IP counsel. We build the slide deck and talk track with you so it's structured, emotional, and converts.
  • Evergreen replay (forever): After day one, the same webinar runs as a high-quality pre-recorded session. Every registrant watches it on-demand or at a scheduled evening slot in their own time zone.
  • Multi-channel nurture: Registrants who don't show get emailed and SMS'd over 7 days with a fresh replay link. No-shows become show-ups; show-ups become bookings.
  • One refresh per month: You run one new live webinar a month — we A/B test topics, openers, and angles to find what converts best for each audience.

Your practice spans trademarks, copyright, anti-counterfeiting and import protection, fashion and entertainment counsel, and influencer and advertising work.
