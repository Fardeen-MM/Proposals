What You Get

✓ Voice AI calls every lead in 60 seconds, 24/7 +

Your old agency called themselves a lead generation company. They weren't. They were a form submission company. Getting someone to fill out a form means nothing if nobody calls them.

How it works:
  • Instant trigger: The moment a lead submits a form — from your landing page, a Facebook ad, or Google — our system fires. Within 60 seconds, they get a call from your Voice AI assistant, calling on behalf of GTA Area Homes.
  • Phone and email verified first: Before the AI calls, the system validates the contact details. No more fake numbers, no more dead ends. Only real people get called.
  • Smart retry logic: No answer? The system waits 25 hours and tries again at a different time. Then again 25 hours later. Three attempts across three days — maximizing your chance of connection without being obnoxious.
  • Parallel SMS: While calling, the AI also texts them: "Hi, this is GTA Area Homes. We just tried calling about your inquiry. Reply YES if now is a good time." Covers people who don't answer unknown numbers.
  • 24/7/365: Leads come in at 11pm on a Sunday? They get called at 11pm on a Sunday. Your competitors wait until Monday morning. You don't.
What it means for you, specifically:

You're one person. You can't chase 30-40 leads a month while showing homes, writing offers, and managing clients. The AI handles the first three touchpoints entirely. By the time you get involved, the conversation has already happened.

Calling within 5 minutes makes you 100x more likely to connect versus calling after 30 minutes. Your old agency wasn't calling within 5 minutes. We call in under 1.

✓ Pre-qualified leads land on your calendar, not in your inbox +

The problem with your last agency wasn't just contact rate. It was that the leads who did connect were low-intent, too far out, or not serious. Qualification fixes this.

What the AI asks every buyer lead:
  • Timeline: Looking to move in 0-3 months, 3-6 months, or just exploring?
  • Budget range: Pre-approved? Have a number in mind?
  • Area preferences: Specific neighbourhoods, commute priorities, must-haves?
  • Current situation: Renting? Selling first? First-time buyer?
  • Agent status: Already working with someone, or open to representation?
What the AI asks every seller lead:
  • Timeline to list: Immediately? 3 months? Just curious about value?
  • Property type and rough size: Context for your prep
  • Situation: Downsizing, upsizing, relocating, investment?
  • Current listing status: Expired? Never listed? Just thinking?
What you get before the consultation:

A complete transcript of the AI conversation, a qualification summary, and the lead's answers to every question — all pushed directly into your CRM before you've said a word. You walk into every meeting already knowing who you're talking to.

No more one-to-two year timelines. We filter those out at the qualification stage. Only leads with a clear intent window get booked.
