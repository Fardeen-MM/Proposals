The Honest Take

You asked specifically: what hasn't worked? Here's the truthful answer.

Where this funnel struggles

The Outland funnel — advertising abroad to bring people into a country — is what we just walked through. That's our strongest play, and we don't have a bad campaign on this side of our business.

Where we've seen campaigns underperform is on Inland services — advertising to people already inside a country trying to extend, change visa class, or stay. That market is significantly more competitive, more expensive per lead, and slower to break even (often 3–4 months before the math turns positive).

If you ever want to expand into Inland services for people already in Australia, expect a different conversation — longer timeline, higher costs, lower margins. We'll be honest with you about it then. For now, the Outland focus we're proposing is the strongest position we can put you in.
